Privacy

What is stored, and where

Your profile, resume file, saved answers, license details and application history are stored in the extension's local storage in your browser. They are not synced to our servers. "Delete all my data" on the profile page removes them.

What leaves your device

  • Import resume (your click): the resume is sent to our API and to Anthropic's Claude to extract profile fields.
  • Draft answers with AI (your click): your profile (without the resume file), the job's title, company and description, the questions, and (so drafts sound like you) up to three of your own earlier answers plus your earlier answer to the same question are sent to our API and to Anthropic's Claude.
  • Tailor resume (your click): your profile, your base resume and the job description you pasted are sent to our API and to Anthropic's Claude. The tailored resume comes back to your browser; you download it yourself.
  • License check: your Pro email and license key, to confirm the subscription.
  • A random install identifier with each request, used only to count free-plan usage.

Our API does not store or log resumes, profiles, questions or answers. Anthropic processes them under its commercial terms.

How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you delete it. "Delete all my data" on the profile page erases your profile, resume, saved answers, license details and application history immediately. Removing the extension erases everything.
  • On our API: resumes, profiles, questions, answers and tailored resumes are processed in memory and not stored. A per-install usage counter (no personal data) is kept for up to 40 days. A yes/no subscription check per email is cached for up to one hour.
  • Stripe keeps payment records as required by law; see Stripe's privacy policy.

Data we do not collect

No analytics, no tracking, no browsing history, no selling of data. We only receive what is listed above, and only when you click.

What the extension does on job sites

It reads the application form to find fields and writes your details into that form only. It never submits a form, and never fills fields outside the application form. It does not read other pages you visit.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see your card details.
